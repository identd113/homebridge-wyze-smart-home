# Release notes: homebridge-wyze-smart-home 2.0.0-beta.4

The changelog is written for people installing the plugin. This page is for whoever ships and supports it: what changed in the code, the exact settings and defaults, what has been checked on real hardware and what has not, and what is known to be unfinished.

**Compared against:** `v2.0.0-beta.3` (`4031e56`, published 2026-10-26), the last published release. `2.0.0-beta.4` is 109 commits and 78 files (+8,107 / -441 lines, not counting documentation and the changelog) on top of it. An earlier `2.0.0-beta.4` was prepared (branch `release/2.0.0-beta.4`) but never published to npm; its number is reused for this release, and its changes are part of this release. Always compare against the last *published* release, not the last branch.

**Pairs with:** `wyze-api@2.0.0-beta.4` (see that repository's `docs/releases/2.0.0-beta.4.md`). `dependencies.wyze-api` is `^2.0.0-beta.4` on purpose: the night vision switches, the camera test page and the Cam Plus and bulk property reads need it, and with an older copy they switch themselves off with one warning.

## Upgrading

1. **Breaking: the Privacy switch is now on when the camera is off.** It used to be on when the camera was on, the opposite of its name. There is no setting to keep the old direction. Any scene, automation or Siri phrase that uses a camera's Privacy switch now does the opposite and must be changed. Say so first in the release text.
2. **New HomeKit tiles appear by themselves.** Each camera whose last week of events shows motion gets a Motion Sensor, one that shows sound gets a Sound Sensor, and one whose events carry Wyze's AI labels gets Person, Vehicle, Pet and Package sensors. They can be turned off per camera on the plugin's settings page. HomeKit may start notifying for them.
3. **The Cameras section is gone from the settings form.** A camera's options (switches and sensors) are on its card on the settings page and are saved by the card's own button. The config keys are unchanged. The form's sections open and close instead of being tabs.
4. **Needs `wyze-api` 2.0.0-beta.4.** The range makes npm install it. With an older copy the bulk property read is skipped (one warning), the Cam Plus plan lines are left out, and the night vision switches and the camera test page switch themselves off with a message.
5. **Homebridge 1.x must be 1.8.5 or newer.** `engines.homebridge` is `^1.8.5 || ^2.0.0` instead of `^1.6.0`: the plugin needs Node 22 and Homebridge 1.x accepts it only from 1.8.5. Not run on a Homebridge 1.x here (the tests ran against Homebridge 2.1.0).
6. **Rollback:** `npm install homebridge-wyze-smart-home@2.0.0-beta.3`. The config keeps working; the new per-camera settings are ignored by beta.3. Cameras then have the old Privacy direction again.

## New settings

Per camera (in the `cameras` list, or on the settings page), and one under `advanced`:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `cameras[].motionSensor` | `auto` | `auto` adds a Motion Sensor when the camera's events show motion; `on` always adds it; `off` never. |
| `cameras[].soundSensor` | `auto` | The same for the Sound Sensor. |
| `cameras[].detections` | empty = automatic | A list such as `["person", "pet"]` for exactly those AI sensors; `["none"]` for none. Face is added only by name. Names the plugin does not know are kept and ignored. |
| `cameras[].garage`, `spotlight`, `floodlight`, `siren`, `notifications`, `motionDetection` | `false` | Extra HomeKit accessories for the camera (as in beta.3); now also ticked on the camera's card. |
| `cameras[].nightVision` | `false` | Two switches, Night Vision and Night Vision Auto, set on the camera over the home network (local TUTK session through `wyze-api`). |
| `advanced.sensorHoldSeconds` | empty = 120 (5 to 3600) | How long a sensor stays on after the event, counted from the event's own time on Wyze's clock. |
| `advanced.webServerPort` | `0` (off) | A port from 1024 to 65535 starts the camera test page inside Homebridge. 1 to 1023 is refused. |

## What changed in the code

### Cameras (`src/accessories/WyzeCamera.js`, `src/camera/*`)

- Motion, Sound and AI-label sensors (`detectionSensors.js`, `sensorHold.js`): a sensor reacts to a new event id whose age is 30 seconds or less, stays on for the hold time and is extended by a newer event. Discovery reads the last week of events, repeats (every 30 minutes while a Motion or Sound sensor is missing, every 6 hours otherwise) and says what it found only when that changes. Motion and Sound ask Wyze for their own event types, so a week of one cannot crowd the other out of the 20 events a request returns.
- One poll for every camera (`motionPoller.js`): the cameras share a request. Wyze returns at most 20 events in all, so when an answer is full the cameras that came back empty are asked for again (up to three rounds), and one busy camera cannot hide another's motion.
- Properties for cameras and lights are read in one request per refresh (`propertyPoller.js`), with the per-device read as the fallback.
- Privacy switch: on means the camera is off. A camera that is switched off shows a black picture with a crossed-out red dot in the Home app instead of its last picture, and the plugin updates its idea of the camera's power as soon as Wyze accepts the switch. `power_switch` also follows the property list, so this works for cameras with an attached accessory.
- Cam Plus plans (`plan.js`): looked up once per start (retried after 10 minutes if it failed), noted for the settings page, and logged: a camera with no plan, a lapsed plan, or one ending within 14 days.

### Night vision (`src/accessories/WyzeCamera.js`)

- Two switches per camera with `nightVision` set: "Night Vision" is on when the camera is in `on` mode, "Night Vision Auto" when it is in `auto`; both off is `off`. Turning a switch on selects its mode; turning one off means `off` only when the camera is in that mode, so a scene that turns everything off cannot switch night vision off from the other mode.
- The state is read once about 20 seconds after start and then every 30 minutes (`wyze-api` `getCameraNightVision`), and read again after every change; a change that the camera does not confirm is an error and the switches go back. With an older `wyze-api` the log says to update, once.
- The camera answers over a local TUTK session, so Homebridge must be on the camera's network; only cameras with the `dtls` flag, an IP and keys in the Wyze device list work. A camera serves a few sessions, so a read or change can interrupt a Wyze app viewer; `wyze-api` keeps one conversation per camera at a time.

### Camera test page (`src/webSettings.js`, `src/webSensors.js`, `src/webPageLink.js`, `src/detectedView.js`)

- `advanced.webServerPort` starts the page from `wyze-api` (`createCameraTestServer`) inside the Homebridge process. The plugin gives it a read-only view of the Advanced options and of each camera's sensors (both read from Homebridge's `config.json` on every request), the list of devices it does not support (`getAccessoryClass` returns nothing), what is known about a model (`plannedDevices.js`), the versions for a report, and the issue form to open.
- The page never writes the config: changes go through the settings page and Homebridge's own UI (`updatePluginConfig` and `savePluginConfig`). The plugin notes the page's links in `homebridge-wyze-smart-home/web-page.json` under Homebridge's storage folder (mode 600; removed at shutdown and at start when the page is off) so the settings page can offer an Open button.
- The Other devices tab runs `wyze-api`'s device report for one unsupported device (read-only: device list, property list, device info, devicemgmt for a camera, optionally a local session) and prefills the new-device form. The form starts on "It is not supported yet" and "Not in this list" because a link cannot choose a dropdown option.

- Unsupported-device catalog: `GW_DBD`, `GW_WC`, `ME_DB1` and `ME_WCO3` (Solar Cam Pan) are in `.github/scripts/device-names.json`, so the new-device form lists them, and `ME_WCO3` has a `src/plannedDevices.js` entry (wyzeapi's open pull request 336 reads it through devicemgmt, but its live view uses Agora, which we do not support). The model code and dates are from other projects and are not checked on hardware. The October 2026 Solar Cam has no known model code and is only in the README table.

### Settings page (`homebridge-ui/`, `src/detectedSensors.js`, `src/detectedView.js`)

- One card per camera holds all of its options: its extra switches as checkboxes and the sensors as pills, with the plan line and the sound-recording warning. A card's changes are saved with the card's own button (a notice says so while there is something unsaved). The page's server (`homebridge-ui/server.js`) holds the logic, in `src/detectedView.js`, so there is one copy: it presents the cameras for the config the page holds and checks a change before the page hands it to Homebridge to save.
- The form's Cameras section was removed (its options are on the cards); the other sections are expandable fieldsets, not a tab strip.

- A "Detected sensors" panel: each camera's sensors as on/off pills with an AI mark on AI labels, its Cam Plus plan, and a line above the cards for each live plan. The Sound pill is greyed out with a warning when the camera's sound recording is off in Wyze. The plugin writes what it finds to `detected-sensors.json` under Homebridge's storage folder; the page only reads it.
- Save changes only what the user changed: `detections` is left alone unless a detection pill was changed, nothing but that camera's keys may differ, and Save stops with a message when the settings form has edits that are not saved yet. Every change is checked first and nothing is saved when a check fails or cannot finish.

### Packaging and project

- `package.json` has a `files` list: with only an `.npmignore`, npm ignores `.gitignore`, so local files such as a Homebridge `config.json` or `persist/` folder would have been packed.
- `CLAUDE.md` and `AGENTS.md` are untracked and ignored. A test fails if one is tracked again or if the package would hold a private file.
- `docs/releasing.md` and a docs index `docs/README.md` are new.

## Quality and care

- **Tests:** 428 tests pass in 38 files, with none skipped. Timing code runs on mocked timers, the settings page logic runs in a `vm`, and the package contents are checked by packing the package.
- **Lint:** ESLint runs on `src/` with zero warnings allowed, in CI as well as locally. It stays on `src/` on purpose.
- **Backward compatibility:** nothing existing was removed. A config written for beta.3 keeps working, the new settings all default to automatic, and rolling back to beta.3 is safe (see Upgrading). The one deliberate break, the Privacy switch direction, is marked **Breaking** in the changelog. The `wyze-api` dependency is checked against its own 1.x compatibility test (see that repository's notes).
- **Review:** two code reviews, one of beta.4's content before it was tagged and one of everything in this release (the test page, night vision, the settings page, the device report and the local camera client). Their findings were fixed; the second found, among others, a race that let two night vision changes run together, a night vision scene bug and a lockout of the page's own user after a restart.
- **Privacy of the repository:** agent files (`CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `.claude/`) are untracked, ignored and left out of the package, and a test fails if that changes.
- **Real hardware:** the final build ran on a test bridge with four cameras and a chime base before release (see below).

## What has been checked, and how

### Checked on the test bridge (real cameras: a Cam v2, two Cam v3 and a doorbell)

- The bulk property poller and the Motion, Sound and AI-label discovery run against the real cameras; the note file records each camera's sensors, sound-recording state and Cam Plus plan, and the plan lines appear in the log (the one covered camera, and the others as "no plan").
- Privacy: setting it powers the camera off or on at Wyze (Wyze reports the power change within seconds and live view is refused while it is off), and the owner confirmed the inverted direction in the Home app.
- Wyze lists events late: a sound at the Garage was seen by the plugin about 8 seconds after Wyze listed it.
- The final build (with the code-review fixes of 2026-11-01) was deployed to the test bridge and started cleanly: no errors or warnings, every camera's Motion and Sound sensors found, the plan lines logged, and all four of the doorbell's AI labels recorded. That run also caught a flaw, fixed in the same build: the label scan read a camera whose newest events were not labelled yet as having no AI detection, so the doorbell (which has all four labels) was recorded with none. It now asks once for any label before deciding.

### Also checked on the test bridge for this release

- The camera test page: it serves the cameras, the other-devices list (five devices of an account) and the read-only settings and sensors views; a report for one unsupported device returned its property list and device info with no MAC address and no IP address in it.
- Night vision over the local session: read and set (with read-back) on a Doorbell v2 and a Cam v2 earlier in development; reads on both Cam v3 cameras.

### Unit tests only

- The settings panel logic (run in a `vm`): saving, the new Save rules, the Sound-recording warning, the plan lines. The sensor hold timing (with mocked timers), repeated discovery, the fair-share polling, the plan retry, the power-state fix, the camera-off picture, the package contents and the private-file guards.

### Not verified on hardware

- The HomeKit night vision switches end to end (the switches in the Home app, a scene, the 30 minute read); the code and its tests are in place, and the config key was found not to reach the camera code until a fix late in this release.
- The camera test page in a browser other than the one the author used, and the settings page's Open button in every browser (a popup blocker may stop it; the address is then offered as text).
- Whether GitHub starts the new-device form's dropdowns as the template now says (it needs the template pushed).
- The final settings page (the Sound-recording warning, the plan lines and the new Save rules) in a real Homebridge UI.
- The camera-off picture in the Home app after the last fix.
- Sensors firing from real motion after discovery was changed to repeat, and Face and other rare AI labels with real data.
- How a camera on a Wyze plan chains events during long motion; the 120 second hold is built for it.

## Known issues and deferred work

- A camera without a Wyze plan gets an event about every 5 minutes, so its Motion and Sound sensors can lag or skip. That is Wyze's cooldown.
- One device missing from the bulk property read makes the whole read fail for that cycle; every device then falls back to its own request, so the saving is lost while that device is in the group.
- A GitHub issue-form dropdown cannot be chosen by a link; the form's defaults stand in for it.
- HomeKit's own camera on/off control is built but held back (branch `feature/native-camera-power`). HomeKit Secure Video is not planned: it needs a permanently open camera connection and a Wyze camera serves one viewer at a time.
